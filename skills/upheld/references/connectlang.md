An earlier Upheld message treated the delivery column too much like a defense link. It also inferred too much from helper code. The [corrected review](https://github.com/yzm1/upheld/blob/9f3e06f5b9ea9d53d1e48860e318068b9df8d978/docs/CONNECTLANG_METHOD_CASE.md) narrows those claims and links the supplied source.

Apply the lesson by tracing calls and assumptions from the promised behavior to the defending mechanism. Identify what implements the behavior, what detects its failure, and what observed challenge supports that detection. Keep the project's original required features visible. Making its register look complete does not complete the project.

Do not infer that Upheld caused these repairs or that this skill would have found them unaided. This case already shaped the guide and belongs outside held-out trials. [The method](method.md) governs evidence claims in new work.
